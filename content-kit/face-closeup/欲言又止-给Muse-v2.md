—— 视频提示词（英文，原样使用）——

Animate image_1. Vertical 9:16, one continuous 10-second shot, locked-off camera, no cuts, real-time speed.

Her face stays calm, soft and almost completely still for the whole shot: forehead smooth, eyebrows relaxed, mouth relaxed, head and shoulders still. Everything happens in her eyes and lips, in very small movements.

0–3 s: She looks at someone just past the camera. Her lips part slightly, as if she is about to say something. She takes a small breath. No words come.
3–6 s: She gently closes her lips and swallows once. Her eyes become glossy, and water gathers along her lower eyelids.
6–8 s: She blinks once, slowly. One small, clear tear slides down from the outer corner of her left eye along her cheek.
8–10 s: She stays still and composed. Her gaze lowers slightly.

Silent: only quiet room tone and her soft breathing. Same face, same light and same framing from the first frame to the last.
